## Gotchas already paid for

- **Server timezone**: next-intl defaulted to the server's zone (UTC on Vercel).
  Fixed with `ViewerTimeZoneProvider` (`useSyncExternalStore`, server zone
  during hydration). Format with explicit fields when adding `timeZoneName`
  (`dateStyle` can't combine with it).
- **`flyToBounds`** picks a long duration for far jumps and hides markers
  meanwhile; pass `duration: 1`.
- **Vercel runtime cache** skips entries over 2 MB; Uganda's snapshot is ~3.5
  MB, so `'use cache: remote'` would not help without splitting it.
- **Prod data facts** (all checked against the API):
    - `collectionDate` is never null and equals `createdAt` in 85–97% of
      Sessions, so "capture date present" is always 100%.
    - Submitted Sessions have `certifiedBy.certifiedAt = null` (all 145
      Jul–Sep), so Time to confirmation reads "Not confirmed" for them.
    - `expectedSpecimens` exists from ~Mar 2026 and is complete from May 2026;
      since then expected ≈ arrived.
    - Sessions with no images are empty traps (0 specimens, 0 expected).
    - Inference durations exist only for Uganda and Ghana images; `capturedAt`
      everywhere.
    - **Site 11 "Other"** (Uganda): test/training catch-all (Kampala/Entebbe,
      Cameroon and Nairobi GPS, devices never used at real Sites, nothing ever
      certified, 176 in Needs Review). Adds 200 specimens in Jan 2026 and 464 in
      Aug 2026 to Uganda totals today.
    - Uganda: 331 Sites = 11 districts × 30 + Other; districts match the team
      sheet roster through Rollout 3. Devices in 2+ districts in one month: 3 of
      272 device-months, all Jan 2026.
- **DHIS2 sync** (`vectorcam-api` `handlers/dhis2/sync.ts`,
  `services/dhis2-aggregation.service.ts`): one event per household (Site) per
  month or per cycle; specimen counts summed over the household's Sessions,
  other fields from the latest Session; no state or type filter; marks all
  grouped Sessions SUBMITTED. `GET /dhis2/sync` needs `collectionCycleId` +
  `siteId` (not allowlisted in this app).
