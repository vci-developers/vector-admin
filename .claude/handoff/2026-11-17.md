      (`[data-map-label="country"]`); touching names ease 4 px apart
      (`LABEL_EASE_PX`).
    - **Click a country** → `country` param; the Program picker stays in step;
      the map frames that country with a fade-through refocus; the surrounding
      world dims (`CountryFocus`). "Return to global view" button top-left.
    - **Return / click outside** (`ClearSelection` → `onReturnToGlobal`) shows
      whenever the view is narrowed: a country, Programs below the default
      (`programsNarrowed`), or the stephensi filter. It clears the country and
      district, resets narrowed Programs, and turns stephensi off.
    - Inside a country: district areas shaded; **names sit on their areas only**
      (no tags or leader lines). Colliding names are dropped, busiest first. The
      chosen country's own name is hidden.
    - **Tooltips are hover-only** (no permanent/sticky ones; user 2026-11-17).
    - Picking an area while no country is chosen also sets its country.
    - **Stephensi filter** (Filters popover): map shows stephensi only. Purple
      shading on areas/countries with it, other areas grey outlines, no scan
      markers, other countries removed, map framed on the stephensi countries.
      The right panel switches to the stephensi chart. Green legend hidden.
      Stephensi dots were removed.
    - **Timeline / Play** (`progress-map-timeline.tsx`, 900 ms per month): map
      shading is cumulative ("Cumulative to …"), so no dips. Colours step in 300
      ms per month (`.districtArea` transition). Countries, and their areas and
      names, appear with their first upload, except a chosen country, which is
      outlined from the start. Play keeps a selected area's view; otherwise it
      flies to frame everything.
- **Right panel**:
    - **Scans per month** (`progress-monthly-chart.tsx`): each bar is scans,
      split by species share of the month's specimens (`SpeciesBar`, one colour
      per species). Calibration specimens and "…other" species get neutrals,
      stephensi gets magenta.
    - Selecting a species (click its row in Species breakdown) brings it to the
      base, fades the rest to 10%, and zooms the axis to that species' specimen
      counts (`focusedAxisMax`). Bars then count specimens, with the remainder
      cut off at the top. The tooltip highlights the focused row.
    - Segments animate via WAAPI from their last drawn state (`Segment`,
      `DrawnSegments`), not CSS transitions.
    - Area picker (`progress-area-picker.tsx`): grouped under country headings,
      indented; search matches area or country; button reads "Area, Country".
    - **Species breakdown** (`progress-species-breakdown.tsx`) is the chart's
      legend: swatch per row, click to focus, "x" replaces the swatch on the
      focused row, hover ring on the swatch.
    - **During Play** the panel is `ProgressPlaybackSummary`: big total, the
      same stacked chart in compact mode (`upTo` month: later bars wait flat and
      grow up), stats, species breakdown.

## Gotchas already paid for

- **Recharts rebuilds bar shapes on every update** (remounts the `<g>`), so CSS
  transitions on segments never run. `Segment` animates with WAAPI from the last
  drawn state, stored per `month|species` in a ref map.
- **Recharts draws one pass with the old axis** before the new one. A glide not
  yet painted (or still in its hold) keeps its original start, or clearing a
  focus flashes solid colour.
- **Firefox backdates an animation's start** to the last painted frame, and a
  month change blocks ~100 ms, so growth looked like a pop. `GROW_HOLD_MS = 120`
  with `fill: 'backwards'` and timing from `animation.ready`.
- **The user has Reduce Motion on**; anything gated on `prefers-reduced-motion`
  silently does nothing for them.
- **Leaflet `setMaxBounds` pans (animated) into the new limit**, landing after
  the footprint refit and carrying the view off-centre (Cameroon).
  `setMaxBoundsQuietly` sets it with `_enforcingBounds`. The limit is the extent
  padded 0.5 so fit margins aren't clamped (Ghana, Colombia sat under the play
  bar).
- **Fit padding**: `overviewFit` / `selectionFit` reserve
  `credit + TIMELINE_CLEARANCE (100)` at the bottom for the play bar and
  attribution.
- **Leaving a wide country** (Colombia frames below zoom 6) collapsed it into
  one solid country node until world data loaded. A layout effect in
  `ReportingFootprint` calls `awaitRefocus` the moment `country` clears.
- **React compiler lint** rejects mutating a hook value inside an effect; move
  the mutation into a module-level function.
- Map label boxes: `placeLabels` is the collision check; `placeDistrictNames`
  still exists but is used only for upcoming tags now.
- **CARTO tiles need an API key now** (watermark); Esri's light-grey base still
  has faint labels. That's why we kept OSM plus the veil.
- `kenya:unknown` ("District not recorded") has no shape; selecting it from the
  dropdown makes the map and panel disagree (see open items).
- Python splices into prettier-formatted TSX broke often; re-read after
  prettier, assert anchors exist before replacing.
